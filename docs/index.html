03 / why it exists

Not another
task runner.

Ginger Tail grew out of an independent AI character called Ginger. The problem was not how to make another model call. It was what should persist when the call ends — and who gets to decide what happens next.

↝

Continuity, not amnesia.

Interests, history, and sourced observations can outlive a single model window. A restart need not erase the agent's past.

∴

A thought is not a post.

Noticing something, thinking about it, wanting to share, and actually acting are separate steps. Silence is allowed.

⌁

The keys stay with the host.

The model can suggest. Only trusted host policy can authorize effects. Uncertain outcomes are not blindly retried.
